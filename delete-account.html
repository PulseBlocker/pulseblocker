Delete your PulseBlocker account

PulseBlocker for Android

You can delete your PulseBlocker account and all the data stored with it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: In the app (immediate)

  1. Open PulseBlocker.
  2. On the Home screen, tap your profile picture in the top-right corner to open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email to pulseblocker@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my PulseBlocker account".

Send it from the email address you use to sign in to PulseBlocker, so we can confirm the account is yours. If you signed in with Google or Microsoft, use the address of that Google or Microsoft account. We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, sign-in details, username and avatar choice.
  • Your synced focus subjects and focus and break sessions.
  • Your synced daily usage summaries and per-app usage.
  • Your synced PulseBlocker settings and the device name saved with them.

What is not deleted

  • Data stored only on your phone, such as your blocklists, schedules and block history. It stays until you clear PulseBlocker's data or uninstall the app. The app keeps working without an account.
  • Problem reports and crash reports you've already sent. These are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically. To have yours removed sooner, mention it in your email.

For more about how PulseBlocker handles data, see our privacy policy.
